Privacy Policy — Opportunities Left

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Opportunities Left is an app for counting what is left of a period you choose. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. Almost everything it knows stays on your device. This page describes the few things that do not.

What stays on your device

Everything you type or choose is stored only on your device, in a folder the app shares with its home screen widget. None of it is uploaded anywhere:

  • The names and notes you give your counters
  • The start and end dates you pick
  • The rule, cell shape, and colour of each counter
  • Any background photo you add, kept as an image file on the device

Deleting the app removes all of it.

What the app sends: usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to answer two questions: whether people who install it go on to make a counter, and whether people who make one go on to add the home screen widget. Two events are recorded.

  • A counter was created. Sent with which of nine fixed rule names it counts by (for example weekdays or publicHolidays), whether its cells stand for days or weeks, and how many counters you have in total.
  • A widget was found on the home screen. Sent once, the first time any is detected, with the widget's size and how many there are.

Nothing you wrote is included. No counter name, no note, no date you picked, no photo, and no free text of any kind ever leaves the device.

Firebase itself also records ordinary technical information with these events: a randomly generated identifier for this installation, the device identifier iOS gives to apps from the same developer, your device model and system version, the approximate country inferred from your network address, and the fact that the app was opened. Google processes this on our behalf; see Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

What the app sends: public holidays

When a counter uses a rule based on public holidays, the app asks the public service at date.nager.at which days are holidays in a given country and year. The request contains only that country code and year. No counter, name, date, or identifier of yours is included.

What the app never does

  • It does not track you. Nothing it sends is combined with data from other companies' apps or websites.
  • It does not read the advertising identifier. The Apple framework that would provide it is not part of the app.
  • It shows no ads and sells no data.
  • It asks for no contacts, location, microphone, or health data. Photos are read only when you pick one yourself, and stay on the device.

Keeping and removing data

Analytics events are retained by Google for up to 14 months and are then deleted automatically. Everything else lives on your device and is gone when you delete the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that would identify anyone of any age.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
